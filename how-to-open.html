How to open VPassFolder

It looks like a calculator. That is deliberate. Here is the way in.

  1. 1

    Open the app. You will see a calculator, and it is a real working one.

  2. 2

    Type your PIN on the keypad, the one you chose when you first set the app up. The digits appear as an ordinary calculation.

  3. 3

    Tap the collapse button in the top left corner — the small four-corner icon that shrinks the calculator into a compact panel.

The vault opens.

If nothing happens

Then the digits you typed were not your PIN. On its own that button only shrinks and restores the calculator, which is exactly what it does on your phone's own calculator. There is no error message by design — a wrong PIN has to look like nothing at all.

Tap the button again to restore the full-size calculator, clear the display with AC, and try the PIN once more. Make sure you tap the collapse icon, not the gear or the history icon beside it.

If you have forgotten the PIN, the files cannot be recovered. Not by you, and not by the developer. The PIN is never stored anywhere — it is what the encryption key is built from. This is the whole point of the app, and it is the one thing that cannot be undone.

Other things worth knowing

The app can appear in your app drawer as "Calculator" instead of its real name — turn that on under Settings inside the vault.

To take a photo back out, long-press it, choose Move to folder or gallery…, and pick Your gallery at the bottom of the list. The app asks whether to keep the encrypted copy or delete it.

Uninstalling the app deletes the vault with it. Use Back up / move to another phone in Settings before you switch phones.
